# Computational Matter

## Visual thesis

Computation becomes a material: a folded lamellar surface, assembled from hundreds of silver contours. The composition pairs monumental, tightly tracked grotesk type with small scientific annotations. Carbon, warm ivory and mineral cobalt provide the entire site palette.

## Signature moment

The hero's computational core slowly breathes and tilts toward pointer movement. Scrolling separates its contours. Project chapters reinterpret that material as multimodal waves, policy layers, parallel frame strips and discrete instruction paths. Contact returns to simple concentric geometry and clear typography.

## Typography and composition

Self-hosted variable Manrope display and body typography, neutral system mono for annotations. A 1440px editorial frame has 48px desktop gutters and 20px mobile gutters. The hero layers a 60% type region against a 55% art region. Projects use a sticky chapter heading and a spacious visual stage; mobile collapses into ordinary document flow.

## Motion and rendering

Shared soft/interface springs, short tactile transitions, slow environment motion. Native scrolling. One lazy Three.js scene uses a custom contour shader and bounded geometry. HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW adapt resolution and contour density; STATIC preserves the server-rendered SVG when reduced motion is requested, motion is paused, or WebGL fails. Render loops stop offscreen and in hidden tabs. All content and links are ordinary DOM.

## Section rhythm

Hero: monumental and layered. Field notes: quiet editorial. Signal: truthful repository observations and native calendar. Work: four individual chapters with procedural SVG studies. Capabilities: four typographic rows. Education: one timeline. OSS: compact evidence. Contact: oversized type with a restrained resolved core.

## Content and deployment

Curated descriptions cite public repository URLs. No fabricated metrics or case studies. GitHub API data is fetched during builds, cached in public/data/github.json, and refreshed by scheduled Pages builds. The secret remains PORTFOLIO_GH_TOKEN.
